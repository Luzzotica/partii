# Privacy

partii runs the store, accounts, cloud saves and online play for the games on it. This page says what we keep and why.

## Your account

- **Guest accounts.** A game makes a guest account the first time you play, tied to a random id stored with the game on your device. No name, email or other details are needed.
- **Signing in.** If you sign in with email, Apple, Google, Discord or Steam, we keep that account's id (and your email, for email sign-in) so you can get back to your games on another device.
- **Purchases.** We keep which games you own and the store's receipt id. Payment details stay with the store you paid through (Stripe, the App Store, Google Play or Steam); we never see card numbers.
- **Cloud saves and shared content.** We keep the saves and levels you make, and what you choose to publish.

## Gameplay statistics

Games on partii can send anonymous statistics so their developers can see what to fix and improve. Games that do have a **Share anonymous stats** switch in their settings. It's on by default, and turning it off stops statistics from being sent.

What's sent:

- **Events.** These are things like starting a session, finishing or failing a level, joining a game, or connecting a phone. Each event has a few details, such as the level number, how long it took, or how many players there were.
- **Device information.** This is the game's random install id, its version, the platform (Mac, Windows, Linux, iPhone, Android or browser), the build, and whether it's the demo or the full game. If you're signed in, your partii account id is included too.
- **Error reports.** When a game crashes or hits an error, it can send the error message, where in the game's code it happened, and what screen or mode you were in.

What isn't kept:

- Your IP address is never stored with statistics. We keep only the country it comes from.
- No contacts, location, advertising ids, or anything from outside the game.

Raw events are deleted after 90 days. Daily totals (how many players played each day) are kept longer. Only the game's developer and partii can see a game's statistics, and we never sell them.

## Questions and deletion

Deleting your account from a game's settings deletes its sign-ins, saves, purchases on partii and published content, and unlinks its statistics, which then belong to no account.
