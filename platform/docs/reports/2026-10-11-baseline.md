## Scenarios

- **open**: both peers straight on the internet
- **home-nat**: both peers behind home routers
- **cgnat**: both peers behind carrier-grade NAT (symmetric, twice)
- **udp-blocked**: the guest's network drops UDP
- **tcp443-only**: the guest's network only lets TCP 443 out
- **lossy**: the guest is on a bad link: 60±20 ms each way, 5% loss
- **network-switch**: the guest switches networks mid-game
- **background**: the guest is frozen for 10 s mid-game

## Notes

- **What was measured.** Today's production partii (Vercel + Supabase REST rooms, 1.5 s signal polling plus the Fly signal gateway, coturn plus Cloudflare TURN), driven by the real `party-kit` TypeScript in headless Chromium: what players ran. Both peers were in Docker on one Mac, behind the scenario's routers, talking to production over the internet.
- **It connects, slowly.** Every trial connected. Players wait about 1.4 s for a room code, then about 5.7 s from pressing Join until the first message gets through (11 s in the slowest 5% on a lossy link).
- **The native client never connected.** `party-kit-native` (webrtc-rs 0.17) never got two peers connected, even both on one Mac: ICE reached `connected`, but the peer connection never did, and it timed out after 15 s (0/2 trials).
- **Disruptions.** A network switch stalled play for up to 5.8 s (slowest 5%). A 10 s freeze recovered every time.
- The raw trials are `raw/2026-10-11-baseline-legacy.jsonl` and `raw/2026-10-11-baseline-legacy-switch.jsonl`. The network-switch rows come from the second file, which was re-run after a harness fix.
