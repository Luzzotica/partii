# Player content policy

This policy covers everything players share through partii games: levels, worlds, replays, and their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails. Games on partii are played by all ages, so content must suit a general audience.

## Not allowed

1. **Sexual content or nudity.** No sexual or sexually suggestive content, nudity, or sexual references in any form, including drawings, pixel art or shapes built out of level pieces. Nothing sexual involving minors, ever; we report it to the authorities.
2. **Graphic violence or gore** beyond what the game itself shows. Cartoon combat that's part of the game is fine.
3. **Hate and harassment.** Nothing that attacks or demeans people for race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age. No hate symbols. No content made to bully, threaten or target a real person.
4. **Personal information.** No one's real name together with where they live, phone numbers, addresses, emails, or other private details, including your own.
5. **Illegal or dangerous content.** Nothing that promotes or depicts illegal acts, self-harm, drugs, or real weapons for sale.
6. **Spam and scams.** No advertising, links, phishing, impersonating partii or the game's developer, or flooding the browse lists with copies.
7. **Other people's work.** Only share what you made, or have permission to share. We remove content when the rights holder asks.
8. **Cheats and exploits.** Nothing that breaks the game for others or uses bugs to harm other players.

Profanity in titles and descriptions counts as not allowed when it is aimed at someone or is sexual.

## What happens

- Everything published is reviewed, usually within minutes. Content that breaks a rule is hidden from everyone; its author still sees it, with the rule it broke.
- Content that several players report is hidden right away until a reviewer looks at it.
- Repeated or serious breaks (any sexual content involving minors, threats) can get all of an account's content removed.
- If you think we got it wrong, contact us below and say which item.

## Reporting

Use **Report** on the item in the game. For anything else, including copyright takedown requests, email **{{CONTACT}}** with the item's share code or link, what's wrong, and (for copyright) proof that you own the work.
